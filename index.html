About Us

Ethiopian Elite Boarding Institution

Higa Model Boarding School is an elite boarding facility designed to shape promising minds into global change agents. Our system fosters a highly organized educational community.

Equipped with male and female dormitories, library systems, chemistry/physics equipment labs, computer resources, and healthy dining services managed under structural shift controllers.
